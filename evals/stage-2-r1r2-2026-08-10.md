## The mechanism is visible in the runs

Runs 3, 4 and 5 explicitly consider Major and choose Minor, in the Phase 6 ethics
row's own vocabulary:

- run 3 — "reporting defect by default per this skill's own escalation framework"
- run 4 — "reporting completeness rather than defects in the core technical claim"
- run 5 — "reporting defect"

and they apply it to faults that are **not** the ethics gap: GISAID-only
deposition, a missing primary citation for the genomic comparator, absent
negative-control results. Phase 6 says of an unstated ethics statement, "Default to
the reporting defect, and state the escalation condition explicitly." That is
deliberate and, for ethics, correct. What the runs show is the pattern
**generalising past ethics** into a broad "reporting-completeness gaps are Minor"
rule that Phase 6 never states.

## What this does and does not establish

**Established:** the ethics row works, now measured without the floor confounding
it. All 5 runs search the full text for approval/consent language and discuss it;
the baseline managed 2 of 5.

**Not established: which change caused the Major drop.** Today's build carries the
ethics row *and* Stage 2, and the ethics row has never been measured alone — the
2026-08-10 re-run shipped it together with the floor, and `rerun-2026-08-10.md`
already flagged that as a confound it intended the next measurement to resolve.
This measurement does not resolve it either, because Stage 2 arrived in between.

The isolating run is cheap and obvious: **R1 against `e4c5ea2`** ("Revert the
peer-review severity floor; keep the ethics row"), the last commit before Stage 0.
Five runs, roughly $12.

- Majors ≈ 1 there → the ethics row generalised, Stage 2 is not implicated, and the
  question becomes whether Phase 6's escalation framework should be scoped to
  ethics explicitly.
- Majors ≈ 6 there → Stage 2's reformulation moved severity grading, and the
  warrant-table and rule-binding rewrite needs re-examining despite the canaries
  passing.

Until that runs, attribution is open, and this file should not be cited as evidence
against either change.
