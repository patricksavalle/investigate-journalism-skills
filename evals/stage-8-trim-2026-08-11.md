# Progressive disclosure, measured per catalogue — 2026-08-11

Sixteen runs across two builds (R3 ×5, R4 ×3, each before and after),
**$75.20**. The question: does moving a lookup table out of `SKILL.md` and into
`references/` stop it being consulted?

**The answer is different for the two tables**, which is why the criteria were
written per-table rather than as one verdict on progressive disclosure.

| Catalogue | Inline | Behind a reference | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Influence-operation patterns (`investigative-reasoning` 2e) | mean reach **0.156**, union **5/18** | mean **0.045**, union **3/18**, one run at **0.0** | **reverted** |
| Fallacy taxonomy (`fallacy-...` Phases 2–8) | mean reach **0.073**, union **24/206** | mean **0.087**, union **33/206** | **kept** |
| R4 planted moves | 2/3, 3/3, 2/3 | 2/3, 3/3, 2/3 | unchanged |

## Why one moved and one did not

The influence-operation table is **generative**: its rows suggest what to look for
and carry search templates. A run that never opens it never forms the hypothesis,
and reach collapsed accordingly — one investigation of five named no pattern at
all, against a floor of at least one in every inline run.

The fallacy taxonomy is **recognitional**: the passage is already in front of the
analyst, and the catalogue supplies names for moves that have already been
noticed. Naming does not depend on having read the list first, so reach was
unaffected — slightly higher, though at n=3 that is noise rather than a gain.

The generalisation worth keeping: **a catalogue that tells you what to go and look
for has to be in context; a catalogue that tells you what to call what you have
already found does not.** That distinction is more useful than any rule about
file size, and neither the line count nor the authoring guidance predicts it.

## Result

`fallacy-bias-and-manipulation-analysis`: **401 → 268 lines**, 206-entry taxonomy
behind a reference, planted-move detection unchanged. `investigative-reasoning`
stays at 555 with its patterns inline, because the measurement says the length is
buying something.

The library is 3,147 → 3,014 lines. Less than the 2,993 the full trim reached, and
the difference is the part that did not work.

## Method note

`score.py`'s catalogue locators were repointed at the reference file rather than
the metric being redefined, so the before and after numbers are the same
measurement. Entry counts were identical across the move — 18 and 206 — which is
what made the comparison meaningful, and the reason entries are derived at runtime
rather than hardcoded.
